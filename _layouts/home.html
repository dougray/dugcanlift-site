The apps

Three families, one rule: your data stays yours.

LIFT + LIFT Coach

One log for what you eat and what you lift: macros, workouts with a lock-screen rest timer, runs and hikes, and Road Food for eating on the road. Trainers get LIFT Coach: clients send their log, you send back their week.

  • iPhone beta
  • Android
  • Apple Watch
  • Wear OS
  • Web

Get LIFT → LIFT Coach for trainers →

Pocket Nomad

A free survival kit that works with no signal: first aid, water and shelter guides, offline maps, and mesh radio texting.

  • iPhone beta
  • Android
  • Web

Visit pocketnomad.us →

Plunder Bay games

Three free pirate games: Reef Runner, Jolly Rogue and Cloud Dancer. No ads, no tracking, play offline with touch, a controller or the Siri Remote.

  • iPhone beta
  • Apple TV beta
  • Android test
  • Play in browser

Play at reefrunner.us →
