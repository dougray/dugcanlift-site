Recipe import tests

Open this page to check the two recipe readers. No build step and no toolchain — the same reason the rest of Coach has none.

These cases are the same fixtures as RecipeJSONLDTests and CaptionRecipeTests in the iOS build, and RecipeJsonLdTest and CaptionRecipeTest in the Android one. Unlike parser.js, these readers owe no cross-client parity contract — they are input paths, and what they produce is indistinguishable from a hand-typed recipe once saved. Running identical fixtures through all three is how they stay recognisably the same feature anyway.
