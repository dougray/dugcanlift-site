I'm someone who looked at a death sentence and said no. 2024 I was told to pickout an electric wheelchair, I decided against that. And instead began my journey, it started fairly basic and much like anything rolling downhill has become bigger. I have made some new friends and lost some along the way. This journey isn't about perfection, because no matter what I will never be and have never been anywhere even close to perfect. It's scary, it's hard, it's dirty, gritty and loud, but that's just a regular tuesday, let's see what a wensday will bring. It's about showing up, and lighting other people up along the way. By choosing to live differently every single day, making little changes we have change our own world along with someone else's as well. How, by proving that all, things are possible with consistancy. I'm here to share the real version of sustainable transformation:the meal plans, the workout splits, the plateaus, and the breakthroughs. Because this road isn't pretty, but it's worth it. And if I can do it, so can you. Let's go get this done together, take that first step... Change Your World!
