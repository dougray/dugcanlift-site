Every macro-friendly Creami recipe on the internet is some version of the same thing: a scoop of vanilla protein, a splash of milk, freeze, spin, done. It works, and I've eaten a lot of it, but "works" and "something I actually want to eat again" aren't the same bar. So I'm starting to build out a rotation of recipes that are actually trying to taste like something specific, not just "cold protein shake."

## The problem with the usual rotation

A base of protein powder and water hits your numbers and nothing else. It's fine for a Tuesday. It's not something you plan your day around. The recipes I actually want are built the other direction — start with a real dessert, carrot cake, cheesecake, whatever, and figure out how to get there inside a macro budget, instead of starting with the protein and hoping it tastes like anything.

## First one: Carrot Cake Cheesecake

This is the one that convinced me the approach works. Three parts — a carrot cake base, a cheesecake swirl, and a toasted pecan crunch — built and layered separately instead of just blended into one flat flavor.

**Creamy Carrot Cake Base**

- 60 g finely shredded carrot
- 1 cup vanilla Fairlife Core Power
- 2 scoops Premier Protein Plus Fiber — Vanilla
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- ¾ teaspoon cinnamon
- ⅛ teaspoon nutmeg
- ⅛ teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons monk fruit sweetener, or to taste

**Cheesecake Frosting Swirl**

- 2 tablespoons light cream cheese
- 1 tablespoon nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1 teaspoon monk fruit sweetener
- ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
- Tiny pinch of salt

**Toasted Pecan Graham Crunch**

- 10 g chopped pecans
- 1 small high-fiber graham cracker sheet, crushed
- Optional ½ teaspoon cinnamon

**Instructions**

1. Toast the pecans in a dry skillet over medium-low heat, 3–5 minutes, stirring frequently, until fragrant and lightly toasted. Don't walk away — they go from toasted to burnt fast.
2. Pull them off the heat and let them cool completely, then chop into small pieces.
3. Finely shred 60 g of carrot. The finer the shred, the smoother the finished ice cream.
4. Add the carrot, Core Power, Premier Protein, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, and monk fruit to a blender and blend until completely smooth.
5. Pour into a Ninja Creami pint, staying below the MAX FILL line, and freeze level for 24 hours.
6. Mix the cream cheese, Greek yogurt, monk fruit, vanilla, and salt until smooth, then refrigerate until needed.
7. Process the frozen base on **Lite Ice Cream**.
8. If it comes out crumbly, add 1 tablespoon Core Power and run **RE-SPIN**.
9. Make a small well in the center of the ice cream and add the cheesecake frosting mixture, toasted pecans, and crushed graham cracker.
10. Run **MIX-IN**.
11. Serve immediately.

**Nutrition (per pint):** 500 kcal · 48 g protein · 38 g total carbs · 17 g fiber · 21 g net carbs · 19 g fat.

## Why bother

The whole reason macros stay sustainable long-term is that hitting them doesn't have to feel like a punishment. A pint that's actually built to taste like carrot cake, and still lands at 48 g of protein for 500 calories, is a much easier habit to keep than another vanilla shake in a bowl. More recipes going up here as I actually make them — the goal is a real rotation, not just this one.
