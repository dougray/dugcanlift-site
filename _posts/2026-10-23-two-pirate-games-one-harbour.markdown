[reefrunner.us](https://reefrunner.us/) used to be one game's front door. It is
now a harbor with two ships tied up in it: **Reef Runner**, the cartoon sailing
game that has lived there since September, and **Jolly Rogue**, a brand-new
pixel-art run-and-gun. Pick one, press Set Sail, and you are playing. Both are
free, both run in the browser, and both now have native apps on the way to
store review.

## The new front page

The home page is split down the middle. Each half dresses as its own game:
Reef Runner gets its sunny cartoon horizon, Jolly Rogue gets a night-blue sea
and square pixel edges. Each half has the game's logo, one line about it, a
**Set Sail** button that goes straight into the game, and a link to that game's
own page if you want to read first.

Nothing moved for anyone who already plays Reef Runner. The game is still at
[reefrunner.us/play/](https://reefrunner.us/play/), so a bookmark or a home-screen
icon still lands in the same place. Its old front page now lives at

## The new game: Jolly Rogue

Jolly Rogue is a side-scrolling run-and-gun in the spirit of the old arcade
classics, with a pirate on foot instead of a soldier. You run right through an
endless voyage across four areas: Palm Tree Island, a merchant ship's deck, the
hold below it and a pirate ship. Each area belongs to a rival crew with its own
colors, and each ends with a boss: the Big Guy, the Captain, Cucumber and the
Whale.

- **Aim in eight directions** and shoot your way through crabs, toothy fish that
  spit pearls, sharpshooting seashells, bomb-throwing starfish, bald brutes and
  cannons you have to duck under. Get close and your blade swings on its own.
- **Every pirate can double jump.** Tap JUMP, then tap it again in the air.
- **Pick up better guns**: a rifle, a three-barrel pistol, a blunderbuss,
  grenadoes and a swivel gun.
- **Three lives and a row of hearts** (six to nine, depending on your class),
  with an extra life for every boss and for every 100 gold you grab in a run.

Gold you keep between runs goes to the **Captain's Quarters**. Play as the
captain or the she-captain, then spend on coats, hats, pants, whole outfits,
blades, guns, a class (Swashbuckler, Brawler or Gunner) and a pet. The parrot
spots treasure ahead; the monkey pockets coins. Beat a boss and the **Black
Market** opens for the run you are in: more hearts, a gun of your choice and a
few other upgrades.

It plays with a keyboard on a computer or by touch on a phone, where a
see-through D-pad sits under your left thumb and JUMP and FIRE sit under your
right. In the browser it plays sideways or upright; held upright, the view
zooms in around your captain so nothing is too small to read. Like Reef Runner,
it saves on your device, plays offline once it has loaded, and has no ads, no
accounts and no tracking.

The pixel art is Pixel Frog's [Treasure Hunters](https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/treasure-hunters)
and [Pirate Bomb](https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/pirate-bomb) packs, released
free for anyone to use. The guns, the parrot and monkey, the hearts and the
she-captain's hair are drawn to match.

## Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Both games have native apps for iPhone, iPad and Android. They are built, they
are running on my own phone for testing, and they are heading into app store
review next. The apps carry the whole game inside them, so they play with no
signal at all. The Jolly Rogue app plays in landscape only, the way a
run-and-gun wants to be held. Reef Runner's app turns with your phone, upright
or sideways.

When they clear review they will be linked from each game's page and from here.
