Earlier today the harbour at [reefrunner.us](https://reefrunner.us/) had two ships. Tonight it
has three. **Cloud Dancer: Beyond the Reef** is live: a steampunk pirate airship shooter, and the
third game in the series after Reef Runner and Jolly Rogue. It's free, it plays in your browser,
and it's built for phones as much as computers.

## Pirates, but in the air

The first two games kept their feet wet. Reef Runner is a one-button sailing game; Jolly Rogue
is a pirate on foot with a gun. Cloud Dancer takes the crew into the sky in a wooden galleon hung
under a brass-ribbed gas envelope, and it plays differently from both: you fly **anywhere**.

- **Drag anywhere to steer.** The airship follows how far your thumb moves rather than sitting
  under it, so your thumb never covers the ship. On a computer it's the arrow keys.
- **The cannons fire on their own.** Your job is where to be, not when to shoot.
- **FULL STEAM** is a ramming dash. You can't be hurt while it lasts, and anything in the way is
  in trouble. It runs on a steam gauge, so it's a tool, not a habit.

## Hide in the clouds

The sky has layers: islands and the sea far below, a band of cloud banks in the middle, and open
sky above. The clouds are the trick. **Duck into a cloud bank and the King's Aerial Navy loses its
aim**: their shots fly straight instead of at you. You lose your aim too, so it's a place to catch
your breath, not to win from.

There are four skies, about a minute each, in an endless loop: the Reef Skies, the Storm Wall
(where lightning flickers a warning before it strikes), the Sky-Forts at sunset, and the Ceiling
of the World. The navy brings gyrocopters, zeppelins, balloon mines, flak towers on the islands
below, armoured sky pikes and cloud lurkers that only show a periscope until they pop out to fire.

## Four bosses

Every sky ends with a boss, and every boss warns you before it strikes.

- **HMS Thunderer**, a dreadnought zeppelin: broadsides, dropped mines and a swarm of gyrocopters.
- **The Tempest Engine**, a lightning coil riding a storm cloud: lightning columns, rings of glowing
  orbs and rapid bursts.
- **Fort Sovereign**, a fortress on a floating rock: turret volleys, mortar shells that burst, and
  slow homing rockets.
- **The Brass Kraken**, a clockwork kraken: tentacles that sweep a whole row of sky after a red
  warning, ink clouds, and a lunge straight at you.

Beat one and you get an extra life, a shower of gold and a weapon crate. Chain shot, a harpoon
that goes through a whole line of enemies, homing rockets and a Gatling gun all turn up in crates.

## Spend the plunder

After each boss the **Sky Market** opens for the run you're in: hull plating, a bigger boiler,
faster cannons, a third cannonball, a lodestone that pulls gold in, a spare crew member, or a gun
of your choice. Gold you don't spend goes into your chest when the run ends.

Between runs, the **Hangar** turns that chest into your airship's look: envelope colours and
patterns, sails, hull paint, a starting gun, and a parrot that fetches nearby gold. Like the other
two games, it saves on your device, plays offline once it has loaded, and has no ads, no accounts
and no tracking.

## Coming to the app stores

Cloud Dancer has native apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, built and tested and waiting alongside
Reef Runner and Jolly Rogue for app store review. The apps carry the whole game, play with no
signal, and are held sideways like a proper shooter. They'll be linked here when they clear review.
