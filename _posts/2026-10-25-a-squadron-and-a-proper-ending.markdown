A few hours after [Jolly Rogue drew its sword]({% post_url 2026-10-25-jolly-rogue-draws-its-sword %}),
two more rounds of changes have shipped to [reefrunner.us](https://reefrunner.us/). Jolly Rogue
got richer, more forgiving and finally has an ending. Cloud Dancer went the other way: it was too
easy, so it got tougher, but now you can fly with a whole squadron.

## Cloud Dancer: build a squadron

The wingman used to be a little rust-red gyrocopter, and you could only have one. Now it's a
small pirate airship, a copy of your own, and the Sky Market sells up to four. They fly in
formation behind you, two close above and below and two further back, each in its own colours
(red, navy, black and gold) and each firing its own cannons. Every one costs more than the last,
so a full squadron is something you save up for.

## Cloud Dancer: tougher seas

After yesterday's bigger rewards, Cloud Dancer got too easy. Every 100 gold was an extra life, and
there was gold everywhere, so you could stack up lives and never really be in danger. So:

- Every 200 gold is now an extra life, not every 100.
- Zeppelins drop three coins instead of six, chests are worth 15 instead of 25 and turn up less,
  and bosses spill 20 coins instead of 40.
- All the scores are halved, so a high score means more than it did.

You'll earn extra lives about a quarter as often as before, which puts the danger back in the sky.

## Jolly Rogue: more gold

Jolly Rogue had the opposite problem. Gold was so scarce you could barely enjoy the Black Market
or the Captain's Quarters. Now every coin is worth two, every sailor drops a coin or two, and
chests, officers, crates and bosses spill about twice as much. That's three to four times the gold
per run. Extra lives now come every 200 gold, so the shops get richer without the game getting
soft.

## Jolly Rogue: Continue?

Losing your last life used to end the run and send you back to the start. Now you get the old
arcade question.

**Yes** puts you back where you fell with three fresh lives, your gold and your gun. **No**
banks your gold into your chest like before. Continues are unlimited, and the game ignores the
first half second so mashing jump while you die doesn't answer for you.

## Jolly Rogue: a proper ending

Jolly Rogue has four areas, each with a boss: Palm Tree Island, the Merchant Ship, the Hold and the
Pirate Ship. After the fourth, the voyage used to just loop back to the start. Now sinking the
Pirate Ship's boss ends the voyage properly.

<figure style="margin:1.5rem 0;">

Gold rains down, you see your run, and you get a **captain's share**: 250 gold for the first
voyage, more for each one after. Then you choose. **Sail On** opens the Black Market and starts the
next voyage, with tougher bosses and new crews. **Retire** banks your gold and takes you home. The
title screen keeps count of how many voyages you've finished.
